Kalaah, since 2018

A quieter kind
of confidence.

Kalaah began with a question: what happens when getting dressed feels less like a decision and more like an expression? We make clothes that leave space for the woman wearing them.

From our studio in Mumbai, we source thoughtfully and make slowly, creating limited collections that reward a second look.

Our philosophy

It is about being remembered.
